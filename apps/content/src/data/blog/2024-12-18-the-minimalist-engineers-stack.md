Minimalism in engineering isn’t just about aesthetics; it’s about reducing the surface area for failure. When we remove accidental complexity, we gain visibility and reliability.

## Why Minimalism?

- Maintainability: Fewer moving parts mean fewer things to break.
- Security: A smaller footprint reduces the attack surface.
- Speed: Lightweight stacks usually perform better and deploy faster.

Focus on the core problem and choose the simplest tool that does the job well.
